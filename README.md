# Earendel

Earendel is a private, self-hosted messaging platform for families and small
close-knit groups who want complete ownership of their communications.

Created as a privacy-first alternative to mainstream messaging services, it
lets you run your own Matrix-based homeserver on infrastructure you control.
Every message, shared file, and piece of metadata stays on your hardware — never
on somebody else's servers.

## Why Earendel?

- **Full data ownership** — all conversations and data reside on infrastructure you own and manage.
- **Private by design** — only your family or group has access; there is no public network or anonymous users.
- **Self-hosted** — deploy on your own machine or a cloud server you control.
- **Standards-based** — built on the open Matrix protocol, giving you interoperability and long-term flexibility.
